This page is a placeholder. It exists so the links on the MediDose concept landing page resolve to something real instead of dropping the reader on a dead anchor. It is not a legal document, it has not been reviewed by a lawyer, and it must be replaced with a real Privacy policy before any data is collected from anyone.

What this page will need to cover

When the early-access form is connected to a real endpoint, this policy must state at least the following before a single submission is accepted:

What is collected

The early-access form asks for a name, an email address, a role, a rough range for the number of daily medications, and optional free-text feedback. The form asks people not to enter medicine names or health details, and the real policy should say what happens if someone does anyway.

What is collected today

Nothing. The published preview does not transmit or store form submissions, does not set cookies, and does not load any third-party analytics or tracking script. Web fonts are requested from Google Fonts, which means Google receives the visitor’s IP address for that request; a real policy needs to disclose that or the fonts need to be self-hosted.

Still to be written

  • How to withdraw consent and unsubscribe
  • Applicable regional rights (for example UK/EU GDPR, CCPA)
  • Whether any of this is treated as health-related data, and the consequences if so
