        The problem

        A pill box works right up until you move away

        Most families manage with a plastic organiser, a phone alarm and memory. That holds until the day changes shape — and it leaves no record at all for the person who no longer lives down the road.

          One system, two apps, two different jobs

              When you live somewhere else

              Close enough to help, far enough to stay out of the way

              Being the one who moved away should not mean phoning every evening to ask about tablets. Your parent decides whether you are involved at all, what you can see, and — separately — whether you can open a compartment from your phone.

                Notifications report what the device recorded — an acknowledgement, or a window that closed without one. Remote release opens a drawer in a house you are not standing in: it cannot tell whether anyone is there or whether the medicine was taken. MediDose does not monitor health, does not respond to emergencies, and is not a safety net.
