# 14 — Growth Strategy

## Guiding principle
On a closed campus, growth is driven by **liquidity → great first experience → word of mouth + referrals**, not paid acquisition. Engineer loops, not campaigns.

## Growth loops

### Loop 1 — Two-sided referral (primary)
- Customer refers customer: both get task credit after the referee's **first completed task** (anti-gaming: completion-gated, not signup-gated).
- Buddy refers Buddy: bonus after referee completes N tasks.
- Buddy ↔ Customer crossover: every Buddy is also a potential customer and vice-versa. Prompt providers to post their own tasks; prompt heavy customers to "earn on the side."
- **Viral mechanic:** the "Share live task" safety link doubles as exposure — friends see the product in a trusted context.

### Loop 2 — Demand-event seasonality
Campus demand is spiky and predictable. Pre-build campaigns around:
- **Hall inspections** → deep-clean push.
- **Exam weeks** → food/laundry/parcel runs ("focus on exams, we'll handle the rest").
- **Move-in / move-out** → moving + luggage + cleaning bundles.
- **Festive/holiday** → airport pickup (phase 2), parcel surges, grocery.
Each event is a re-activation moment; schedule push/email to dormant users.

### Loop 3 — Provider supply flywheel
More reliable Buddies → faster fill → better customer experience → more demand → more earnings → attracts more Buddies. Protect it with onboarding quality bar, ratings, and earnings transparency.

### Loop 4 — Content / social proof
- Campus IG/TikTok: "day in the life of a Buddy," earnings screenshots, satisfied-customer clips.
- Leaderboards / "Top Buddy of the week" (opt-in) for status-driven engagement.

## Activation & retention
- **Activation = first completed task.** Reduce time-to-first-task: pre-filled quick-post buttons, first-task credit, guaranteed fill.
- **Habit formation:** weekly "what do you need this week?" nudge; recurring task templates (post-MVP); reminders before known events.
- **Provider retention:** instant/fast payouts, clear earnings, streak bonuses, fair dispute handling, surge windows.
- **Win-back:** dormant-user campaigns tied to demand events.

## Expansion sequencing
1. **Hall-by-hall within NTU** (cluster → cluster) until campus saturation.
2. **Category expansion** as trust/safety controls mature (T3 categories).
3. **Adjacent universities** (NUS, SMU, SUTD) — replicate the cluster playbook; verification per-school domains; the product is multi-tenant-ready (school field on user/category).
4. **Polytechnics & student-dense areas** later.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Over-subsidizing demand before supply exists (burns cash, breaks experience).
- Expanding geography before liquidity (spreads density too thin).
- Turning on high-risk categories before safety/insurance is ready (one incident can end the company on a campus).
- Vanity metrics (signups) over the North Star (completed tasks/week) and fill rate.

## Measurement
- Funnel: visit → verified → first task posted/accepted → first completion → repeat.
- Cohort retention (weekly) split by role and by hall.
- Referral K-factor; demand-event lift; fill rate and time-to-accept as leading indicators of health.
