# CampusBuddy — The Million-Dollar Plan (Investor View)

> Companion to `venture-brief.md`. This frames the *upside* path using proven
> overseas comparables. Figures on comps are public/illustrative — verify before
> quoting in a raise.

## Thesis (one line)
A **verified-student platform** where everyday help builds the audience, **move-out
& storage drives high-ticket revenue**, and the **verified-student network monetises
via brand deals + ads.** Each piece is already a proven business overseas — nobody
runs the *integrated* version in Southeast Asia yet.

## Founder decisions that shape this plan
- **Moving/shift stays labour-only** — no storage facility, no logistics pivot. This
  keeps CampusBuddy a pure student-help marketplace, but it also **removes the
  storage-margin that the "S$1M-in-a-year" path relied on.** Treat that number as an
  optional-future stretch (only if a self-storage referral partner is added), not the
  base plan.
- **Study help / tutoring added** as a high-value, recurring, on-brand vertical —
  better per-hour economics and repeat use than micro-errands.

## It's proven abroad — this de-risks the idea

|---|---|---|---|
| **Storage Scholars** | US | Student **move-out + storage** is a real, fundable business | 2 student founders → ~US$1.86M (2022) → **US$8M across 175 campuses (2024)**, US$10M+ projected; raised US$250k from Mark Cuban (Shark Tank) |
| **CollegeBoxes** | US | Student storage/shipping is acquisition-worthy | **Acquired by U-Haul** |
| **UNiDAYS** | UK | **Verified-student audience → brand revenue** | ~29M verified students, 115 markets |
| **Student Beans** | UK | Same, monetised via brand campaigns/affiliate | 30M+ verified students globally |
| **TaskRabbit** | US | Errand marketplaces are exit-worthy | **Acquired by IKEA** |
| **Airtasker** | AU | Task marketplaces become real companies | **Publicly listed (ASX)** |
| **Fizz** | US | Verified, campus-by-campus rollout + monetising students | VC-backed, fast multi-campus growth |

**Takeaway:** the two biggest money machines in the student space are **(1) move-out/
storage logistics** and **(2) verified-student brand networks.** CampusBuddy already
contains both — we just need to lead with them.

## The three revenue engines
1. **Everyday marketplace** (laundry, errands) — *not the profit centre.* It's the
   cheap **engagement engine** that builds the verified base and the weekly habit.
2. **Move-out & storage** — *the revenue spearhead.* High-ticket (S$100–250/student/
   break), seasonal, asset-light if partnered with self-storage. This is the
   Storage-Scholars play.
3. **Verified-student brand network** — *the scale engine.* Brands (banks, telcos,
   F&B, tuition) pay to reach verified students via deals, affiliate, and ads. This
   is the UNiDAYS/Student Beans play.

## Path to the first US$1M
- **Storage-led (fastest):** 2,000 students × S$150 × 2 breaks/yr = **~S$600k/yr** from
  storage alone across a few campuses. Storage Scholars hit US$1.86M early doing
  exactly this. Storage gets you most of the way to S$1M on its own.
- **+ Brand/affiliate** once tens of thousands are verified: brand campaigns +
  affiliate commissions layer on (UNiDAYS model).
- **+ Marketplace commission & ads:** supplementary.
- **Verdict:** **S$1M ARR is plausible in ~2–3 years if you lead with storage**, not
  laundry commission. A **S$1M+ valuation** is reachable much sooner — Storage
  Scholars was valued ~US$2.5M with early traction; a working product + a real
  pilot + the storage line is a fundable seed story.

## Why us / the moat
Copyable idea ⇒ the moat is execution: **verification + ratings (trust)**,
**university/hall relationships** (sanctioned access others can't get), **local
density** (win one campus completely), and the **integrated** play (utility +
storage + brand network in one verified app).

## Expansion (where the bigger number is)
Singapore (~250k tertiary students) gets you to a strong S$1M-ish business + likely
acquisition. The **>S$10M outcome needs regional expansion** — Malaysia, then wider
SEA/India — using the same campus-by-campus playbook the US comps used to reach
hundreds of campuses. Demand travels; the comps prove it.

## Risks (and the honest read)
- **SG size caps a pure-domestic outcome** → plan regional early.
- **Storage = real operations** (space, logistics, insurance, breakage) → go
  asset-light via a self-storage partner.
- **Copycats** (it's proven) → speed + university lock-in are the defence.
- **Regulation** (MOM work-pass, hostel rules, SFA, PDPA) → launch with a
  university's blessing; lead with low-risk services.

## The ask / next 6–12 months
1. **Pilot move-out storage at the next semester break** — the highest-signal
   revenue proof (and the most Shark-Tank-fundable line).
2. **Sign one self-storage partner** (asset-light) + 2–3 brand/F&B deals.
3. **Win one campus** on engagement (laundry habit) to seed the verified network.
4. With that traction: raise a small seed / apply for Startup SG Founder, or pursue
   a strategic partner (storage co, student-network player).

## Can it "touch a million" in 12 months? (honest)
Three different meanings — be precise:
- **S$1M net profit in year 1:** **no.** Not realistic for a first-time student team.
- **S$1M net revenue in year 1:** **very hard** — needs ~16k+ storage jobs; that's
  multi-campus at scale with capital and an ops team.
- **S$1M GMV / annual run-rate in year 1:** **the achievable stretch goal.** Storage
  is high-ticket, so it doesn't need huge user counts.

### The only path that gets there: storage-led, two move-out seasons
Singapore has **two move-out spikes a year** (end of each semester). That's two shots
at big revenue in 12 months.
- Math: **~5,500 storage jobs/yr × ~S$180 = ~S$1M GMV.** Split over two seasons =
  ~2,750/season. Across NUS+NTU+others (~80k students) that's **~3–4% penetration
  per season** — aggressive but not crazy *if* trust + ops hold.
- Of that GMV, CampusBuddy **keeps a real margin on storage** (you provide the
  service, not a 5% match): e.g. charge S$180, costs ~S$120 → **~S$60 kept** → ~S$330k
  net at 5,500 jobs. So **S$1M GMV ≈ low-six-figure net** in year 1 — still a great
  outcome, and a fundable one.

### What it would take (non-negotiables)
1. **Lead with storage, not laundry.** Laundry/errands can't touch S$1M in a year;
   storage can.
2. **An asset-light self-storage partner** signed before the first move-out.
3. **A real ops team** (not a solo side-project) — drivers/movers, logistics, comms.
4. **Some working capital** (storage space, insurance, labour float).
5. **Hit BOTH move-out seasons** — miss one and the year-1 target is gone.
6. **2–4 campuses**, not one — single-campus storage won't reach S$1M.

### Honest odds
Touching **S$1M GMV in 12 months is a ~2/10 base-rate stretch** for a first-time
team, but the **storage-led plan is the only version with any real shot** — and even
"missing" it (say S$300–500k GMV) is a strong, fundable result. Chase the S$1M as a
North Star; bank the pilot proof first so you can raise the capital/team needed to
actually attempt the full run.
