# Positioning

## One-liner
**On-demand campus help — by students, for students, cheaper.**
A verified-student marketplace for campus chores and errands.

## The pitch
Post a task — laundry, parcel runs, a spare home-cooked meal, room/storage moving, cleaning — and a
**verified student from your campus** gets it done. On demand, fairly priced, with
ratings and built-in safety. No owing anyone a favour.

## The three things we win on
1. **Verified.** Students-only, gated by campus email + matric verification, with an
   arrival check-in (NTU pass / live matric photo) so the person who shows up is the
   person you accepted. Informal channels can't match this.
2. **On demand.** A buddy is available when you need one — you don't wait on a friend
   who might be busy, say no, or flake.
3. **No social debt.** Paying a stranger a fair price means **no owing favours, no
   guilt, no awkwardness** — and you can use it as often as you like. This is the real
   reason people choose a paid service over "just ask a friend."

## Who we're really competing with (and how we beat them)
| Alternative | Why it falls short | How we win |
|---|---|---|
| **Asking a friend (free)** | Not always available; you owe them; can't ask repeatedly; they flake | On-demand + no social debt + reliability |
| **Free Telegram "favour" posts** | Read as sketchy/scam; unreliable; no accountability | Verified identity + ratings + tracked, accountable flow |
| **Paid Telegram / Carousell gigs** | No identity assurance, no safety, no recourse | Verified students, safety layer, dispute path |
| **Professional services / agencies** | Expensive; not campus-native; overkill for a S$10 errand | Cheaper, student-to-student, hyper-local |

> Key insight: demand already exists (people pay for informal gigs today). We're not
> creating demand — we're **moving existing informal demand onto a trusted, reliable
> rail.** The bar is being *more trustworthy than Telegram* and *more reliable than a
> friend*.

## What we are NOT
- Not a professional/licensed services company (buddies are students, not contractors).
- Not a payments/escrow business (commission is light; revenue is advertising — see `13`).
- Not "cheap = sketchy": trust and safety are the product, not an add-on.

## Brand tone
Friendly, student-native, trustworthy. "Buddy", not "worker". Safety and verification
are stated plainly and often — they are the reason to choose us.
